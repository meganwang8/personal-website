From 2023 to 2026, I played on Brown Quiz Bowl's team and served as president and captain for two years. Now that I have graduated, I play open tournaments in the greater Boston area and staff local tournaments in New England. I also write and edit tournaments and am committed to highlighting topics and figures that are historically underrepresented in the quiz bowl canon.
